AI today

Where AI stands now: what it does well, its limits and open questions.

What AI does well

  • Recognising patterns in images, speech and text.
  • Drafting, summarising and translating language.
  • Writing and explaining code.
  • Helping scientists, for example by predicting protein structures.

Where it falls short

  • Language models can state false things confidently, often called hallucination.
  • Systems can reflect biases present in their training data.
  • Training large models takes a lot of data, computing power and energy.
  • Performance can be uneven: a system can excel at one task and fail at a similar one.

Open questions

  • How can we check that AI systems are reliable and safe?
  • How should AI be governed, and who is responsible when it errs?
  • How will it change work and education?
  • How far can current methods go, and what else is needed?
