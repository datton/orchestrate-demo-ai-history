Timeline

Key moments in AI, decade by decade, from the 1940s to now.

  1. 1943

    Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts publish a mathematical model of an artificial neuron, an early link between brains and computation.

  2. 1950

    Alan Turing publishes "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" and proposes the imitation game, now called the Turing test.

  3. 1956

    The Dartmouth workshop brings researchers together, and the term "artificial intelligence" is put forward by John McCarthy and colleagues.

  4. 1958

    Frank Rosenblatt demonstrates the perceptron, an early learning machine inspired by neurons.

  5. 1966

    Joseph Weizenbaum's ELIZA chatbot shows how a simple program can seem to hold a conversation.

  6. 1970s

    Progress falls short of early promises and funding shrinks: the first "AI winter".

  7. 1980s

    Expert systems, programs built on hand-written rules, bring a commercial boom, followed by a second slowdown.

  8. 1986

    David Rumelhart, Geoffrey Hinton and Ronald Williams publish work that popularises backpropagation for training neural networks.

  9. 1997

    IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov in a match.

  10. 2012

    AlexNet, a deep neural network, wins the ImageNet image recognition contest by a wide margin and launches the deep learning era.

  11. 2016

    DeepMind's AlphaGo defeats Go champion Lee Sedol, four games to one.

  12. 2017

    Researchers at Google publish "Attention Is All You Need", introducing the Transformer architecture.

  13. 2022

    OpenAI releases ChatGPT in November, bringing conversational AI to a mass audience.
