Key people

The researchers and thinkers who shaped the field.

  • Alan Turing

    British mathematician who laid theoretical foundations of computing and asked in 1950 whether machines can think.

  • John McCarthy

    Helped organise the Dartmouth workshop, put forward the name "artificial intelligence" and created the Lisp programming language.

  • Marvin Minsky

    Co-founded the MIT AI laboratory and wrote influential early work on machine intelligence.

  • Allen Newell and Herbert Simon

    Built the Logic Theorist, an early program that proved mathematical theorems, and studied human problem solving.

  • Frank Rosenblatt

    Created the perceptron, an early trainable neural network.

  • Joseph Weizenbaum

    Created ELIZA and later wrote thoughtfully about the limits and risks of computers in society.

  • Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun and Yoshua Bengio

    Pioneers of deep learning who shared the 2018 Turing Award.

  • Fei-Fei Li

    Led the creation of ImageNet, the large image dataset that helped drive the deep learning breakthrough.

  • Demis Hassabis

    Co-founder of DeepMind, whose work includes AlphaGo and AlphaFold.
