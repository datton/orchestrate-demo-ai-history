The history of AI

A short tour of artificial intelligence from its beginnings to today.

Why this story matters

Artificial intelligence began as a bold idea in the 1940s and 1950s: that a machine might one day learn, reason and talk. The path since has been uneven, with bursts of excitement, long quiet stretches known as "AI winters", and a sudden acceleration in the 2010s when more data, faster chips and better methods came together.

This site walks through that story in four short pages.
